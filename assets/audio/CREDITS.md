## Narration

Synthesized with [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) TTS, voice
`en_US-amy-medium`.

| File | Line |
|---|---|
| `hold_message.wav` | "This call requires another participant to continue holding the line." |
| `connecting.wav` | "One moment please, your call is being connected." |
| `not_in_service.wav` | "We're sorry. The number you have dialed is not in service. Please hang up and try again." |

## Sound effects

Dial tone, touch-tone dialing, ringback, hangup click and busy tone are
generated tones. `rain_city_loop.wav` is the ambience mixed under live
conversation.
